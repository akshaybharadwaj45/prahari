Research Gap in Base Paper Why Prior Literature Failed Prahari Lean Solution
1. Temporal Blindness (1 CDM at TCA) Kalyanaraman et al. (2026) discarded 7 days of CDM history and only looked at the final snapshot at TCA, giving 0 hours of lead time. Tracks the full 7-day arc, extracting risk drift velocity (d_risk/dt), giving 3 to 5 days of early warning lead time.
2. 85% False Alarm Rate (Precision = 15%) Synthetic oversampling (SMOTE) caused 6 false alarms for every 1 real collision, which would rapidly deplete satellite propellant. Replaced SMOTE with class-weighted asymmetric loss, achieving 92.09% Precision (slashing false alarms by >5×).
3. Toy Dataset (Only 6 Risky Events) Kalyanaraman et al. trained on only 6 risky events, making their 100% recall a statistical overfit. Evaluated on the official ESA Kelvins test set of 2,167 events (178 confirmed high-risk collisions).
4. Non-Physical Coordinate Splits Prior models treated x, y, z coordinates as arbitrary tabular numbers without orbital invariants. Engineered 3D Covariance Ellipsoid Overlap (miss/σ_3D) and Collision Kinetic Energy (0.5 v²).
